About Vagas Pra Mim

Vagas Pra Mim is a platform created to make it easier for professionals to find open job opportunities across multiple companies in one place. Our goal is to simplify your job search by curating reliable and up-to-date job listings.

Our Mission

To connect talented professionals with companies looking for their skills, using technology to make the process faster and more transparent.

What We Do

  • Aggregate and organize public job openings from verified sources;
  • Offer a clean, mobile-friendly browsing experience;
  • Provide accurate, fresh links directly to the companies' official application pages.

Transparency and Ethics

We respect users' privacy and comply with AdSense, GDPR, and LGPD standards. Our website never asks for unnecessary personal information or charges any fees for job access.
